Take Two Toads · M2 · art test

The look of the finished book

Three drawings in the style planned for the finished game: a plant plate, Old Hessa and the brass scales. Each one has the prototype drawing under it, so you can compare. Look at them on your phone, then answer the three questions at the end.

❧

I.A botanical plate

Ink lines now swell and thin like a quill's, shade is cross-hatched, and the colour is a watercolour wash that misses the line a little, with paper showing through.

The prototype's Emberroot, for comparison.

❧

II.Old Hessa

The first customer, a kind widow with a cold cough. Villagers are the riskiest part of the art, so she is drawn as a simple woodcut. Tap a face to see her three moods.

The prototype's Hessa, for comparison.

❧

III.The brass scales

Heat, Spirit and Weight, each with its own shape in the round badge as well as its own colour. The plumb is where the brew is now; the gold diamond is where the cure needs it. Drop an ingredient in to see the Heat plumb swing.

The prototype's Heat scale, for comparison.

❧

IV.Three questions

  1. Does it all look like one hand-made book, or does something stand out as wrong?
  2. Does Hessa look warm and alive, or stiff?
  3. On the scales, can you see at a glance where the plumb is and where it should go?

Answer in the chat, for example "1 yes, 2 a bit stiff, 3 yes". Anything else you would change is welcome too: colours, line thickness, how busy it looks.

The fonts come from Google Fonts on this test page only. The game itself will carry its own copy, so it keeps working offline.

Drawn in code, no images. Nothing traced.
